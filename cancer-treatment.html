What is Cancer?

Cancer is a group of diseases in which abnormal cells grow and spread in the body. Early detection and the right treatment can improve outcomes and quality of life.

Types of Cancer Treatments

India offers advanced and personalized treatment options using modern technology and a multidisciplinary approach.

Surgery

Removal of cancerous tumors and affected tissue.

Chemotherapy

Uses powerful medications to kill cancer cells.

Radiation Therapy

High-energy rays to destroy cancer cells.

Immunotherapy

Helps the immune system fight cancer.

Targeted Therapy

Targets specific molecules involved in cancer growth.

Hormone Therapy

Used for hormone-responsive cancers like breast and prostate.

When is Cancer Treatment Required?

  • Abnormal growth or tumor detected
  • Persistent and unexplained symptoms
  • Cancer confirmed through tests (biopsy, scan, etc.)
  • Cancer spread to nearby or distant organs
  • Recurrence after previous treatment
  • Need for specialized or advanced treatment options
  • Second opinion for better treatment plan
  • For patients seeking advanced care and better survival outcomes
