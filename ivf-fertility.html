Treatment Overview

IVF (In Vitro Fertilization) is an advanced fertility treatment that helps individuals and couples overcome infertility and achieve their dream of parenthood. It involves fertilising the egg with sperm in a laboratory and then transferring the embryo to the uterus. India offers high success rates, experienced specialists and affordable treatment options, making it a preferred destination for IVF treatment.

Types of IVF & Fertility Treatments

IVF (In Vitro
Fertilization)

Most common and effective fertility treatment for various infertility issues.

ICSI
(Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)

Recommended for male infertility or low sperm count.

IUI
(Intrauterine Insemination)

A simpler fertility treatment suitable for mild infertility cases.

Egg
Donation

An option for women with low egg quality or reduced egg reserve.

Embryo
Freezing

Preserve embryos for future use.

Fertility
Preservation

Ideal for individuals planning to delay parenthood.

When is IVF Treatment Required?

  • Blocked or damaged fallopian tubes
  • Low sperm count or poor sperm quality
  • Age-related fertility decline
  • Unexplained infertility
  • Endometriosis or PCOS
  • Repeated miscarriages
  • Genetic disorders
  • For same-sex couples or single parents
