Patient Stories FAQs

Are these real patient stories?

Yes, all stories and testimonials featured on our platform are from verified international patients who traveled to India for medical procedures with Adam Vacations. Every patient journey is documented with explicit written consent.

Can I speak to a previous patient?

Yes! With consent from our former patients, we can connect you with someone from your country who underwent a similar procedure so you can hear about their hospital experience, doctor care, and recovery firsthand.

How can I share my own story?

If you had treatment in India through Adam Vacations, you can click the “Share Your Story” button above, contact your dedicated care manager, or email us at medical@adamvacations.com. We would be honored to feature your journey!

Will my personal information be shared?

No. We adhere to strict patient confidentiality, GDPR, and international data privacy protocols. Only details, photos, or video materials that you explicitly approve in writing are ever shared on our website.
