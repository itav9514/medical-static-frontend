What is Hair Transplant?

Hair transplant is a minimally invasive procedure that moves healthy hair follicles from a donor area to areas with thinning or no hair. It provides a natural-looking, permanent solution for hair loss and helps restore a fuller head of hair and improve self-confidence.

Read More

When may it be required?

  • Male or female pattern baldness
  • Thinning hair or receding hairline
  • Bald patches due to genetics
  • Hair loss after injury or surgery
  • Loss of hair due to hormonal changes
  • Unsatisfactory results from other treatments
  • To improve hair density and hairline

Frequently Asked Questions

Ideal candidates are men and women with pattern baldness, thinning hair, or hair loss who have sufficient healthy donor hair on the back or sides of the scalp. A detailed scalp evaluation determines suitability.
Hair transplant in India performed by board-certified plastic surgeons and dermatologists has a graft survival success rate of 90% to 95%, delivering natural-looking, lifelong results.
Yes, hair follicles transplanted from the permanent donor zone (back and sides of the head) are genetically resistant to DHT (the hormone causing hair loss) and continue to grow naturally for a lifetime.
A typical hair transplant session takes between 4 to 8 hours depending on the number of grafts (usually 1,500 to 4,000 grafts). It is performed under local anesthesia as an outpatient day-care procedure.
Most international patients can resume light desk work and normal daily activities within 2 to 3 days. Strenuous workouts, swimming, and heavy physical strain should be avoided for 2 to 3 weeks.
Temporary side effects are minimal and include mild scalp tenderness, minor swelling around the forehead, and tiny scabs that shed naturally within 7 to 10 days with post-procedure care.
