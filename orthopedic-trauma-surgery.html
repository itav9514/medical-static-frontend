What is Orthopedic Trauma Surgery?

Orthopedic trauma surgery focuses on treating complex injuries to the bones, joints, ligaments, tendons and muscles caused by accidents, falls, sports injuries or other high-impact trauma. It helps restore function, reduce pain and enable patients to return to their normal activities as quickly and safely as possible.

When is it required?

Severe fractures or multiple bone injuries
Joint dislocations and ligament tears
Sports injuries
Road traffic accident injuries
Complex pelvic and acetabular fractures
Spine trauma
Open fractures and soft tissue damage
