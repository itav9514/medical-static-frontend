What Is Spine Surgery?

Spine surgery is a specialized treatment option for selected conditions affecting the vertebrae, discs, nerves or spinal stability. It aims to relieve pain, improve function and prevent further complications when non-surgical treatments are not effective.

Read More

When may it be considered?

  • Herniated disc causing persistent pain
  • Spinal stenosis (narrowing of the spinal canal)
  • Spinal deformity (such as scoliosis)
  • Spinal instability
  • Selected fractures of the spine
  • Nerve compression causing pain, numbness or weakness (doctor evaluation required)

Types of Spine Surgery

Depending on the condition, different surgical procedures may be recommended. The most suitable option is decided after medical evaluation.

Discectomy

Removal of damaged disc material to relieve nerve pressure.

Spinal
Decompression

Creates more space for nerves by removing pressure.

Spinal
Fusion

Stabilizes the spine by joining two or more vertebrae.

Minimally Invasive
Spine Surgery

Uses smaller incisions for faster recovery where appropriate.

Spinal Deformity
Correction

Treats conditions like scoliosis or other spinal deformities.
