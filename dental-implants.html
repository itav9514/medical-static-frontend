What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a modern and long-lasting solution for missing teeth. They are artificial tooth roots, usually made of titanium, that are placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth, restoring both function and natural appearance.

Read More

When may dental implants be recommended?

  • Missing one or more teeth
  • Difficulty in chewing or speaking
  • To improve smile and facial appearance
  • When dentures are not suitable
  • To prevent bone loss in the jaw
  • For a long-term and stable tooth replacement solution
