What are Neuro Surgeries?

Neuro surgeries include a range of advanced surgical procedures to treat conditions of the brain, spine and nervous system. These surgeries are performed by experienced neurosurgeons using modern technology to achieve better outcomes and improve quality of life for international patients.

Read More

When May Neuro Surgery
Be Required?

Brain or spine tumors
Spinal cord compression
Aneurysm or vascular malformations
Epilepsy not controlled by medication
Chronic back or neck pain
Neurological conditions affecting movement, sensation or function

Types of Neuro Surgeries

Different procedures are available, and the right option depends on your medical condition and doctor's recommendation.

View All Procedures
Brain Tumor Surgery

Removal of benign or malignant brain tumors using advanced techniques.

Spine Surgery

Treatment for slipped disc, spinal stenosis and other spine conditions.

Aneurysm Surgery

Treatment for brain aneurysms and vascular malformations.

Epilepsy Surgery

Surgery for drug-resistant epilepsy to reduce or stop seizures.

Deep Brain Stimulation

Advanced treatment for Parkinson's disease and movement disorders.
