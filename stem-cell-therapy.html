What is Stem Cell Therapy?

Stem cell therapy uses the body's own cells or donor cells to support repair, regeneration and immune regulation. It is being studied for various medical conditions and is offered in selected cases as part of modern treatment approaches. The suitability, evidence and expected outcomes depend on the specific condition and type of therapy, and should be discussed with a qualified specialist.
