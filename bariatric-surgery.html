What is Bariatric Surgery?

Bariatric surgery includes a range of weight-loss procedures that help reduce stomach size or change the digestive process. It is generally recommended for individuals with obesity or weight-related health conditions when lifestyle changes alone have not been effective.

Read More

When Bariatric Surgery
May Be Required?

Obesity with weight-related health issues
Difficulty losing weight through diet and exercise
Conditions such as type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, sleep apnea
Recommended by a qualified doctor after medical evaluation

Types of Bariatric Surgery

Different procedures are available, and the right option depends on your medical condition and doctor's recommendation.

View All Procedures
Sleeve Gastrectomy

A procedure where a portion of the stomach is removed, reducing its size.

Gastric Bypass

Creates a smaller stomach pouch and reroutes the digestive system.

Mini Gastric Bypass

A simplified version of gastric bypass with a single connection to the small intestine.

Other Bariatric Options

Includes adjustable gastric band, revisional surgery and other specialized procedures.
