What is Eye Surgery?

Eye surgery includes a range of advanced procedures to treat vision problems and eye conditions. It can help improve vision, reduce dependence on glasses or contact lenses and treat various eye diseases. In India, international patients can access world-class eye care with experienced specialists and modern technology.

Read More

When may it be required?

  • Blurry or reduced vision
  • Dependence on glasses or contact lenses
  • Cataract or cloudy vision
  • Glaucoma or high eye pressure
  • Cornea damage or irregular shape
  • Retinal problems
  • Other eye conditions affecting daily life

India offers comprehensive eye surgery procedures including LASIK (PRK, SMILE, Contoura Vision), advanced cataract surgery with premium monofocal and multifocal IOLs, ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens) for high refractive errors, glaucoma surgeries (trabeculectomy and laser iridotomy), corneal transplants (DALK, DSEK, PKP), and vitreo-retinal procedures.
The right eye surgery depends on a comprehensive preoperative ophthalmic examination, including corneal topography, pachymetry, optical coherence tomography (OCT), and dilated retinal check. Leading eye specialists in India evaluate your age, lifestyle, visual needs, and eye health to recommend the safest option.
Yes, eye surgery in India is performed at internationally accredited eye hospitals (JCI and NABH) using US-FDA approved technologies such as Femtosecond lasers, advanced phacoemulsification systems, and high-definition operating microscopes with very high clinical success rates.
For refractive procedures like LASIK or SMILE, a stay of 4 to 6 days is usually sufficient. Cataract surgery typically requires 5 to 7 days per eye or both eyes with spacing. Complex retinal or corneal transplant procedures may require 10 to 14 days for initial healing and follow-up.
Most LASIK and cataract surgery patients can resume non-strenuous daily activities and light office work within 24 to 48 hours. Rubbing eyes, swimming, contact sports, and heavy lifting should be avoided for 2 to 4 weeks as per your doctor's instructions.
