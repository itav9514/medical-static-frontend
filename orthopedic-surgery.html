What is Orthopedic Surgery?

Orthopedic surgery focuses on treating conditions related to the bones, joints, ligaments, tendons and muscles. It helps restore mobility, reduce pain and improve quality of life using advanced surgical and non-surgical treatment options.

Read More

When may it be needed?

Severe joint pain or stiffness
Fractures or complex bone injuries
Arthritis and worn-out joints
Sports injuries
Spinal conditions
Deformities or restricted movement
When other treatments don't provide relief

Types of Orthopedic Surgery

Orthopedic surgery includes a wide range of procedures based on the condition and severity of the injury.

Knee Replacement

Restores mobility and reduces pain

Hip Replacement

Treats hip joint damage and arthritis

Arthroscopy

Minimally invasive diagnostic and treatment

Fracture & Trauma Care

Treats complex fractures and injuries

Spine Surgery

Treats spinal disorders and nerve compression
