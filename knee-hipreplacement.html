What is Knee Replacement?

Knee replacement is a surgical procedure to replace damaged or worn-out knee joint with an artificial implant. It may be recommended for severe arthritis, injury or other conditions when non-surgical treatments no longer provide relief.

Read More

When may it be required?

Serve knee pain
Arthritis
Reduced mobility
Knee injury
Deformity or joint damage

Types of Treatment

Knee replacement can be performed using different techniques based on the patient's condition, age and activity level.

Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

Replaces the entire knee joint

Partial Knee Replacement (UKR)

Replaces only the damaged part

Minimally Invasive Knee Replacement

Smaller incision, faster recovery
